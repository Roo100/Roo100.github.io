About the Prototype

A student-centered college-navigation concept.

This independent creative submission shows how undocumented and immigrant students could use connected tools to organize the postsecondary process.

About this project

Built around student choice, privacy, and practical planning.

Pathway to College is an interactive college-list and action-planning prototype created for a 2026–2027 fellowship application. It is designed to help undocumented and immigrant students explore colleges, organize applications, prepare financial questions, compare options, and plan enrollment.

The visual system is inspired by John Jay College’s navy and cyan identity. This independent creative submission is not an official John Jay College or CUNY publication.

About this prototype:Pathway to College demonstrates how an interactive, multilingual college-navigation platform could be developed before or during the fellowship. College, scholarship, deadline, and support examples illustrate possible functions; a future version could incorporate verified information from schools, community-based organizations, and college partners.
Purpose

A tool for student decision-making

The site helps students produce a concrete college list, personal action plan, and timeline rather than only reading general information.

Privacy

No account required

The prototype does not request a name, address, or immigration status. Saved lists and plans remain in the user’s browser unless the browser data is cleared.

Future development

Built to expand

A program version could incorporate verified college data, scholarship tracking, school-specific calendars, and additional planning and progress tools.
