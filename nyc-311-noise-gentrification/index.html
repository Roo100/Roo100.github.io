How it can encourage gentrification

Noise complaints can route state attention toward nightlife, street activity, neighbors, and businesses. In areas already facing rising rents, that attention can support enforcement patterns, nuisance narratives, landlord pressure, and policing strategies that make long-term residents and local cultural life easier to displace. This visualizer treats 311 calls as one signal in a wider housing and governance process, not as proof of individual intent.

Sources and limits
