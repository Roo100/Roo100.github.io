7 in 10: NYS Incarcerated Mothers Dashboard

Confirmed NYS DOCCS counts only (2014-2021). This dashboard centers one core pattern: about 7 in 10 women in custody are mothers, with year-to-year variation and changing overall custody totals.

Why this series stops in 2021: DOCCS has not produced the post-2021 published Table 7 series used for this dashboard’s mothers-count measure. The project therefore ends at the last produced/confirmed year in that sequence (2021) and does not estimate later values.

Core Finding

About 7 in 10 women in NY custody are mothers

Source Documents

Method note: Mothers counts come from NYS DOCCS Under Custody Report Table 7 (number of living children by sex). Age and sentence context fields are from the same report year tables for women. The displayed series ends at 2021 because DOCCS has not produced the subsequent published Table 7 series used by this dashboard. No modeled estimates are used.
