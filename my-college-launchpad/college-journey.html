Junior year

Explore and prepare

Use this stage to understand postsecondary options and establish a manageable starting point.

My actions

  • Consider interests, strengths, and possible fields of study.
  • Review graduation requirements and current academic progress.
  • Begin a list of colleges, programs, or training pathways.

Research and support steps

  • Learn the major postsecondary pathways.
  • Write research questions for each option.
  • Set a date to review the first college list.

Documents to organize

  • Unofficial transcript or course list
  • Activities and interests
  • Questions about college vocabulary

Questions to answer

  • What would you like college to make possible?
  • What practical factors matter most to you and your family?
