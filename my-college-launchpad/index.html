College preparation essentials

Five areas students can organize with an Advocate.

The prototype brings together the milestones, opportunities, referrals, mentoring supports, and practical strategies students may need throughout the college process.

01

Milestones and Deadlines

Review the college journey, application stages, required materials, and dates that need follow-up.

02

Scholarship Opportunities

Build a scholarship search plan, organize eligibility questions, and track application deadlines.

03

Legal-Assistance Resources

Prepare questions and connect with qualified legal-service providers without asking an Advocate to give legal advice.

04

Mentorship Programs

Identify Peer Advocates, school counselors, campus programs, and community mentors who can provide continued support.

05

Tips for Common Challenges

Break down barriers involving costs, missing materials, unfamiliar terms, time pressure, and enrollment steps.
