Student Resource Guide

Five parts of your college plan.

Review milestones and deadlines, scholarship opportunities, legal-assistance resources, mentorship programs, and common challenges with a Peer Advocate.

Milestones and Deadlines

Scholarship Opportunities

Legal-Assistance Resources

Prepare questions and connect with a qualified legal-service provider. Peer Advocates make referrals; they do not give legal advice.

 04

Mentorship Programs

Identify Peer Advocates, school counselors, campus programs, and community mentors who can provide continued support.

 05

Tips for Common Challenges

Break costs, missing materials, language barriers, time pressure, and enrollment steps into one manageable next action.
Legal-assistance referral

Refer rather than advise.

A student can write a general question without entering immigration status or unnecessary identifying information. The Advocate can help locate an approved provider, prepare questions, and set a follow-up date.

Referral pathway:Student identifies a legal question → Advocate helps locate a qualified provider → Student contacts the provider directly → Student and Advocate follow up on affected college-planning tasks.
