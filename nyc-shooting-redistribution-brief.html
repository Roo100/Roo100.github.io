Research Brief: Uneven Public Safety Gains: A Data Brief on NYC Shooting Redistribution, 2021-2025

This brief uses public NYPD shooting incident data, neighborhood boundaries, demographic context, and spatial analysis to examine where shootings declined, where they persisted, and which neighborhoods remained relatively burdened after the post-pandemic shooting surge.

The brief should be read alongside the interactive map and scatter plot dashboard, which allows users to compare baseline and comparison years, filter by borough or change category, and hover over neighborhoods for contextual data.
