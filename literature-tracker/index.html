From Plantation to Prison

A Black Feminist Phenomenological Study of Black Women, Criminalization, and Reproductive Control

This literature tracker organizes peer-reviewed journal articles for a Black feminist phenomenological study on formerly incarcerated Black women, pregnancy, criminalization, and reproductive control. The tracker follows a formal article-review structure: problem, purpose, sample, method, findings, limitations, and relevance to the study.
